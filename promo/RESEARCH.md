# MineMyLead 60s promo: research (Oct 17, 2026)

## 1. Structures that sell (30-60 s tool ads)
- **Hook in 1-3 s, value prop by ~6 s.** TikTok: 90% of ad-recall impact happens in the first 6 s; use a hook, body and close structure ([TikTok Creative Codes](https://www.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads)). Meta-focused guides say to hook in 1-2 s, with no logo intro ([Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/blog/facebook-video-ads-best-practices/), [Stackmatix](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/meta-video-ads)).
- **Google/YouTube ABCD** (Attract, Brand, Connect, Direct): 2+ shots in the first 5 s, brand or product in the first 5 s, one focused message, a specific CTA, more direct toward the end ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551), [ABCD playbook PDF](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/8472/ABCD_Complete_V7b_HR_1.pdf)).
- **Show the product in use.** TikTok-cited data: +25% recall and +65% brand affinity when the product is on screen ([Wistia 2024 summary](https://wistia.com/blog/insights-state-of-video-report)).
- **Length.** Wistia (13M+ videos): videos under 1 min average about 50-52% engagement. Put the CTA at the end for videos under 5 min ([Wistia](https://wistia.com/blog/optimal-video-length)). Cold paid ads work best at 15-30 s, so make the 60 s the master and cut 30 s and 15 s versions from it ([Polimake](https://polimake.com/en-us/kb/video-length)).
- **Formats that work for tools:**
  - Show the result first: open on the finished outcome (a lead with proof), then explain.
  - PAS: problem, agitate, solution.
  - Before/after: cold chasing vs. warm replies.
  - Demo-led: real UI, zoomed in, cursor-driven.

  For MineMyLead, combine show-the-result-first with PAS and a demo.

## 2. Comparable examples
- Apollo, "How to use Apollo.io in less than 2 minutes": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bnr5Xqzr-Ec. A presenter on camera plus fast UI cuts. Each feature gets a one-line benefit, and it closes on a "create a free account" CTA. Lesson: one benefit per screen.
- Brand24 channel: https://www.youtube.com/c/brand24/videos. Social listening explainers built on animated mention cards popping in, the same visual idea as our "someone just asked for you" cards.
- Ramper (BR, PT): https://www.youtube.com/@Ramper/videos (e.g. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W-5N-81KBF4). Mostly long demos. Shows the BR B2B tone: direct, practical, plenty of "você".
- Lemlist, Clay, Gong, Hunter, Mention, RD Station: their live paid ads are best browsed in the [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) and the [TikTok Creative Center top ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads/). I could not verify specific ad URLs from here, so I'm not inventing any. Common patterns: founder or face hooks (Lemlist), a "this is magic" result reveal (Clay), and a data-claim hook (Gong).

## 3. Practical rules
- **Captions are always burned in.** Most feed video is watched muted (figures quoted run 70-85%). Captions should carry the message on their own, not just transcribe the voice.
- **Aspect ratios:** 9:16 master for Reels/Shorts/TikTok, with key content in the centre safe zone and nothing in the bottom ~20% or right edge. Use 16:9 for YouTube and the landing page, and 1:1 or 4:5 for the feed. Design 9:16-safe so it can be re-cropped.
- **Pacing:** a visual change every 1-2 s in the hook, 2-3 s during the demo (people need time to read the UI).
- **On-screen text:** 3-7 words per card, at most 2 lines, held at least 1.5 s. Captions lead the voice slightly.
- **Voiceover + music beats music-only** for explaining a new category. Keep the voice short and punchy, and duck the music under it.
- **Music:** upbeat electronic/pop or a light Brazilian groove (funk-pop or bossa-house feel), 110-128 BPM. Use a rise or drop at the reveal (~6-8 s) and at the CTA.
- **Brand:** small logo or watermark from second 1, name said by ~5 s, full logo plus URL on the end card.
- **CTA:** a soft mention mid-video and a hard CTA in the last 6-8 s: one action, URL on screen.

## 4. Copy angles
- "Pare de correr atrás de lead frio. Fale com quem já pediu." / "Stop chasing cold leads. Talk to people who already asked."
- Intent: "Tem gente pedindo o que você vende agora, nos comentários."
- Proof/trust: "Cada lead com o link do comentário e, quando possível, um print." (Matches the current site claims.)
- Control and safety: "Só lê. Ritmo humano. Nada sai sem a sua aprovação."
- Single payment: "Pague uma vez. Sem mensalidade." Brazilians are tired of subscriptions. Mention parcelamento only if it really exists.
- Privacy: "Roda no seu computador. Seus leads são seus."
- Free trial: "3 leads grátis do seu nicho" (wait list).
- **Brazil tone:** informal "você", warm and direct, a little humour. Use real micro-niches (lojinha de roupa, dedetização, curso online) and mention Instagram strongly, since it's the main sales channel in BR. People expect a quick, personal conversation, but **don't claim WhatsApp features**. Saying "mensagem pronta pra você enviar" is fine. Price: say "pagamento único" and avoid unexplained "R$ 990" shock; anchor against a monthly subscription if needed.
- **EN:** tighter and more benefit-driven. Say "one-time payment", not "lifetime".
- Avoid: "compradores garantidos", unverified numbers, and implying automated sending.

## 5. Recommended 60 s beat sheet (9:16 master, ~120 BPM)
| Time | Visual | VO / on-screen text (PT; EN in the final cut) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3 | A real-looking comment pops up: "Alguém indica uma loja de roupa?" Highlight pulses | "Alguém acabou de pedir o que você vende." |
| 3-6 | Comments multiply on YouTube, X and Instagram cards | "E você nem viu." |
| 6-10 | Pain: owner scrolling, cold DMs left on "visto" | "Chega de correr atrás de lead frio." |
| 10-13 | Beat drop, MineMyLead logo snaps in | "MineMyLead acha quem já está pedindo." |
| 13-18 | Demo: type "o que você vende" + city | "Diga o que você vende." |
| 18-26 | Search runs, lead cards stack | "Ele busca nos comentários do YouTube, X e Instagram." |
| 26-33 | Zoom into one lead: link + screenshot | "Cada lead com prova: link e print." |
| 33-40 | Suggested message, "Aprovar" button clicked | "Mensagem pronta. Só sai com a sua aprovação." |
| 40-46 | Before/after split: cold list vs. warm replies | "Fale com quem já quer comprar." (or "...quem já pediu") |
| 46-52 | Three icons: one-time payment, runs on your computer, read-only | "Pague uma vez. Sem mensalidade. Privado." |
| 52-60 | End card: logo, minemylead.com, button | "Entre na lista e ganhe 3 leads grátis." |

**Hook options:**
1. "Alguém acabou de pedir o que você vende."
2. "Você está perdendo clientes nos comentários."
3. "Pare de caçar cliente. Tem gente te procurando."

EN: "Someone just asked for what you sell."

**Cuts:** 30 s (0-13, 26-40, 52-60) and 15 s (0-6, 33-40, 52-60).
