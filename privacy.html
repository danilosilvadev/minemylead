Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026

What we collect from you

Only what you send us: your email address and a short description of what you sell. We use it to reply, scope your batch and deliver it. We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Where lead data comes from

The leads we deliver come from public sources: GitHub, Hacker News, YouTube, Substack, DEV Community, X, Google Maps business listings and public feedback, public Instagram posts and reels, and Google Search results. Maps feedback is read as a signal about the business; we do not use reviewers as leads. Contact links come from public profiles and websites. We do not buy scraped email lists and we do not access anything private.

Removal

If you appear in our data and want to be removed, write to contact@minemylead.com with a link to your public profile. We remove you and stop including you in batches.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. If you pick a language, your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage and never sent to us. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Questions about your data: contact@minemylead.com.
