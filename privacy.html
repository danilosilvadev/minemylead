Site visitors

If your browser’s preferred language is Portuguese, or you previously chose Portuguese, the English pages can send you to the matching Portuguese page. A choice of English or Portuguese is stored in this browser under the key mml_lang. It stays on your device. We do not receive it.

This site does not set its own cookies. It does not run an analytics script or an advertising script. Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files from Google, so Google receives the request, including the IP address the request comes from. Google’s own policy applies to that request.

Buyers and the order form

If you send the buy form, you give your email, a short description of what you sell and to whom, and the product you chose: niche pack, taste, or another niche or a refresh.

  • When no Stripe Payment Link is configured, the page opens your email app with a message to contact@minemylead.com. The message goes through your own email provider. We receive it if you send it.
  • When a Stripe Payment Link is configured, the page also opens Stripe Checkout and adds your email and a product reference (pack, taste, or refresh) to that link. Stripe processes the payment and collects the payment details. We do not receive your full card number. Stripe’s privacy policy applies to what Stripe collects.
  • The checkout file has a slot for a form address that would receive the same fields as JSON (email, brief, offer, and product code). That address is empty in the published file, so the site does not post the order to a form service today.

We use an order to reply, take the one-time payment, scope the niche, deliver the file, apply the taste credit, replace a lead, and answer support. We do not sell that information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Onboarding after payment

Stripe Payment Links are set up to send buyers to /onboarding/ or /pt/onboarding/. The form asks for:

  • full name, work email, company, and website
  • what you sell, in one sentence, and an optional price
  • best customers, buyer role, company size, industry, and region
  • the pain in the buyer’s words, the public channels to read, an optional other channel, and who to exclude
  • an optional note on what separates an A lead from a C lead
  • which public fields you need: name, profile link, company, the public post showing the pain, public email, public phone
  • optional notes
  • the Stripe checkout session id, if it is in the page address; the plan (pack or taste when present); the language (en or pt); and the time you submitted

A hidden field, website_url_confirm, is a spam trap. If it is filled, the server keeps nothing and still answers as if the form succeeded.

Until you press send, answers you have typed can stay in this browser under the key mml_onboarding_v1, so a refresh does not clear the form. They are not sent to us until you submit. After a successful submit, the page removes that local copy.

The form posts JSON to the Supabase Edge Function onboarding-intake on the Supabase project minemylead. The project notes place that database in us-east-1 (United States). Rows go in the table onboarding_clients. The browser cannot read that table. Row access from the public site is closed.

When email sending is turned on, that function uses Resend to email you a confirmation and to email the operator a copy of the submission. The default from-address in the project notes is onboarding@minemylead.com. If the email key is not set, the row is still stored and the email is skipped. The confirmation describes the same delivery steps as the onboarding page.

Lead data from public sources

Packs are built from publicly available posts and comments. The sources named on the site are GitHub, Hacker News, YouTube, Substack, DEV Community, X, Google Maps business listings and public feedback, public Instagram posts and reels, and Google Search results. A lead can include the quote, the source link, a score from 0 to 100, and public contact links. Maps feedback is a signal about the business. We do not use reviewers as leads. We do not buy scraped email lists, and we do not access private accounts or private messages.

The pack you buy is a SQLite file you own and keep on your machine. We do not host that copy. We keep the lead data we used to build packs so we can deliver an order, replace a lead that misses the brief, and honor a removal request.

After the file is on your machine, you decide how you use it. The terms of service say you follow the anti-spam laws and platform rules that apply to your outreach, and you stop when someone asks you to stop.

The free desktop app

The app runs on your machine. The sample leads ship inside the download. Your pack stays on your machine. We do not receive that file back, and we do not send messages for you.

The site describes optional language tools (Codex, Claude Code, Gemini, Grok, and Cursor Composer) as command-line tools you are already signed into on that machine. If you use one, the prompts leave your machine under your own account with that provider. MineMyLead does not sit on that path. This website does not store an API key for those tools.

Why we use the data

We use order and onboarding data to perform the purchase you asked for: reply, charge once through Stripe when you pay by card, scope the niche, deliver the SQLite file, credit a taste toward the first pack within 14 days of delivery, replace a lead, and answer support. We use public-source lead data to build that file, to replace a lead, and to take someone out when they ask.

Your rights under the GDPR and the LGPD

If the GDPR applies to you, you can ask us to confirm whether we process personal data about you and to give you access to it. You can ask us to correct it, erase it, or restrict it, and you can ask for a portable copy of data you provided. You can object to processing that is based on legitimate interest, including being included in a lead pack, if that is the basis the operator records above. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw that consent. You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU or EEA country where you live, where you work, or where you believe an issue happened.

If the LGPD applies to you, you can ask for confirmation that we process your data, access to it, correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data, and anonymization, blocking, or deletion of data that is unnecessary, excessive, or processed in a way the law does not allow. You can ask for portability, and for information about the private and public entities we shared the data with. You can ask us to delete data processed on the basis of consent. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest, including a lead pack, when that is the basis. You can petition the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD) about your complaint.

Email support@minemylead.com to use any of these rights. Tell us the email you used to order, or send a link to the public post, so we can find the record. We do not ask you to pay a fee unless a law that applies allows a fee because the request is unfounded or excessive. If we cannot delete something because we still need it to finish a purchase, to replace a lead, or because a law requires us to keep it, we will say so.

How to ask to be removed from lead data

If you appear in our lead data and want to be removed, email support@minemylead.com with a link to your public profile or to the source post. We remove you from the lead data we still hold, and we do not include you in packs we deliver after we process the request.

We do not host the SQLite file a buyer already keeps. We cannot delete that copy from their machine. The terms require the buyer to stop contacting someone who asks them to stop.
