Straight answers

What we will not promise.

Will you find thousands of leads?

No. In most niches a few hundred people describe the problem clearly. We tell you the real number first.

Where does the data come from?

Nine public sources, listed in Method above. Nothing private, no scraped email lists.

Does this work for my niche?

It works where your buyers talk in public. If they do not, we tell you on the first call.

How do you make sure the leads are real?

Each lead links to its proof, and we audit samples by hand. A lead that fails gets replaced.

Do you send the messages for me?

No. Chat writes drafts from the leads already in your pack. You send every message. There is no automatic DM, email, or InMail.

Does chat find new leads?

No. Chat is a reader. It consults the sample leads or the SQLite pack you already have. It does not search the web and it does not mine leads from the app.

Can I use the app without buying a pack?

Yes. The Linux and Windows apps are free to download, with about 10 sample leads to consult. The full niche pack is a separate one-time purchase.

Is this a subscription?

No. The niche pack is $199 once. You keep the SQLite file. There is no monthly charge. Buy another niche or a refresh when you want more.

How do I pay?

Send the buy form with your niche. Payment is one time, for the pack or the taste you chose. We reply with a secure payment link. Nothing on this page starts a monthly bill.

What if the pack is not good?

Leads that miss the brief are replaced. We do not refund a delivered pack or taste; we fix the file.

What if I need more than one niche?

Each niche is its own $199 pack, and so is a refresh of a niche you already bought. The $49 taste is 10 leads for one niche, credited toward your first pack within 14 days. A larger volume is a custom quote. Write to us.

Can someone ask to be removed?

Yes. Ask and we remove it.
