Straight answers

What we will not promise.

Will you find thousands of leads?

No, and anyone who says yes is selling a list. In most niches a few hundred people describe the problem clearly. We tell you the real number before you pay for more.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources across the internet: open forums, threads, comment sections and public profiles. We use what people published themselves. We do not sell scraped email lists and we do not touch anything private.

Does this work for my niche?

It works where your buyers talk in public: software, B2B products, communities with open threads. If your customers never write about the problem online, we will tell you on the first call.

Do you contact the leads for me?

No. You write to them, in your voice. We give you the reason to.

How do you make sure the leads are real?

Every lead is qualified by AI and audited by hand on samples, and each one carries a link to its proof. A lead that does not check out gets replaced.

Is this a tool I have to learn?

No. It is a service. You send a brief and get a finished batch back.

Can someone ask to be removed?

Yes. Anyone can ask us to remove their data, and we do it.
