Straight answers

Straight answers.

Where do the contacts come from?

From the public CNPJ registry of the Receita Federal. Each contact shows its source and type, and each message says where it came from and how to stop receiving it.

Do the contacts reach the owner?

Not always. We are measuring this number in the pilot and will publish the result.

How do you choose the companies?

From the registry: activity, size and location. Then we check that the company sells to other companies. Those that do not fit are left out.

Do you send messages for me?

No. You approve each message and send it from your own WhatsApp or email.

How much does it cost?

The pilot has a price agreed with you. The price after the pilot comes from the measured results.

Can I ask to be removed?

Yes. Write to contact@minemylead.com. We stop contacting the company and it is blocked from every future pack.
