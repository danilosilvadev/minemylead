How it works

Find. Verify. You approve.

01 FIND

The extension finds people

The Chrome extension uses the accounts you are already signed in to on YouTube, X, Instagram and email. No platform APIs.

02 VERIFY

Only real buyers

Fixed filters and free AI verification remove promos, bots and questions outside your type of business. 90%+ precision in testing.

03 APPROVE

You decide what goes out

The app writes the message in the lead's language. It lands in an approval queue: approve, edit or skip. Sending happens in the extension, on your computer.
