# What to charge for a niche pack of hot and super hot leads: research, 2026-11-01

Status: research from public web pages found on 2026-11-01 (vendor pages, vendor blogs, comparison sites). Nearly every number is a vendor's own claim, not an audited market price. Where a number could not be found, it says so. No buyer has paid for our leads yet, so everything here is a hypothesis to test.

## 1. What we sell (measured on our own data, 2026-11-01)
- **Super hot**: a real small or mid-size business in the niche and market, not a chain or a non-profit, with a stated owner profile page (LinkedIn, Instagram or X). **Hot**: the same without the owner page.
- We deliver a **public profile link**, not a verified email or phone number.
- Quality (blind check, one labeller): 21 of 23 companies the gate called hot were real leads, 2 unsure, 0 clearly wrong (28-company fresh sample). Small sample.
- Cost to produce: local machine, no API bill. Current yield about 7 super hot per 100 owner-finding searches; about 130 searches an hour; 100 super hot takes roughly 6-8 hours of machine time.

## 4. What a buyer gets for the money: cost per reply
The buyer's real question is what each reply costs. Benchmarks found: personalized outbound sequences get **3-8%** replies, generic ones under 1% (Portuguese outbound guide on snov.io). A buyer's alternatives for finding customers: dental Google Ads leads cost **$50-$85** each, $150-$300 per new patient (webtonic, delmain and others); agencies charge $50-$600 per qualified lead.

If our super hot lead lets the buyer send a personalized message to the owner:

| Price per lead | at 3% reply | at 5% reply | at 8% reply |
|---|---|---|---|
| $1.00 (R$ 5) | $33 per reply | $20 | $12.50 |
| $2.00 (about $199 per 100) | $67 | $40 | $25 |
| $4.00 (about $399 per 100) | $133 | $80 | $50 |

A price that keeps the cost per reply below paid acquisition ($50-$85) at a middling 3-5% reply rate is **about $1.50-$2.50 a lead**. $4 a lead only works if replies are 8% or better. **The reply rate to our leads has not been measured**; it is the number that decides the price.

## 5. Conclusion
- **Defensible price now: about $199 per 100 super hot (R$ 990 in Brazil), roughly $2 a lead.** That is 5-20 times the price of raw contact data (justified by the fit check and the found owner), and 25-150 times cheaper than a qualified-lead agency (justified because we do not book meetings or give verified emails).
- **$399 per 100 is not supported by the data.** It needs a reply rate of 8% or more, or a delivered email and phone.
- A pack of hot leads without owner pages is a weaker product: about $1 a lead or less ($99 per 100).
- Raise prices only with evidence: measured reply rate from the first buyers, a larger blind quality check with a second labeller, and an email or phone number delivered for the owner.

## 6. How to learn the real price
1. Use the free 3-lead trial and the promo codes. Ask each trial user what they would pay for 100.
2. Sell the first 10 packs at $199 / R$ 990 with a replace-any-invalid-lead promise.
3. Ask each buyer for their reply count after two weeks. The price follows the measured cost per reply.
4. EU/UK: the owner details of sole traders are personal data under GDPR. Get a lawyer's view before selling there (Brazil's LGPD was cleared by your lawyer).
