The product

A niche pack is a one-time purchase of a SQLite file you own. It holds about 100 leads graded A–C for one niche you describe. “About 100” is the shape of a pack. It is not a promise of an exact count. Each lead includes:

  • a quote: the person’s words from a public post or comment
  • a link to that public source
  • a fit score from 0 to 100, with the reason for the score
  • public contact links, such as public profiles and channels, including a public LinkedIn link when that is the public contact

You own the file. It stays on your machine. We do not host it for you. Chat in the desktop app reads the file you already have and writes drafts only. You send every message. There is no automatic email, DM, or InMail. The app does not search for new leads and it does not mine from the app.

Another niche, or a refresh of a niche you already bought, is another one-time pack at the same price, unless we quote a different volume in writing before you pay.

The onboarding page also describes a CSV delivered with the pack. The file you own is the SQLite pack.

The free app and the paid taste

The Linux and Windows app is free to download. It runs on your machine and includes about 10 sample leads so you can try the consult. Those samples are not a niche pack, and they are not the paid taste. Chat consults the samples, or the pack you already have. It does not mine new leads.

The taste is a separate one-time purchase: 10 leads scoped to your niche. It is not the free sample inside the app.

Where the lead data comes from

Leads are built from publicly available posts and comments. The sources named on the site are GitHub (issues and discussions), Hacker News (posts and comments), YouTube (video comments), Substack (posts and comments), DEV Community (articles and comments), X (public posts), Google Maps (business listings and public feedback), Instagram (public posts and reels), and Google Search (public posts and forum threads). Public feedback on Google Maps is read as a signal about the business. Reviewers are not used as leads. Contact links come from public profiles and websites. We do not buy scraped email lists, and we do not access private accounts, private messages, or other non-public data.

A public post is not permission for every later use. Your outreach still has to follow the acceptable-use section and the laws that apply to you.

We drop bots, spam, staff, and duplicates, score what remains from 0 to 100 against your buyer, and check source links. Samples are audited by hand. That process reduces noise. It does not guarantee that every lead is still current, or that the person wants to hear from you.
