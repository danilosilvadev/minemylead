What you buy

A fixed batch of qualified leads for one niche: the public quote, a link to its source, a score and public contact channels for each lead, delivered as a CSV and a local viewer. The taste batch is 10 leads. The pilot batch is 100 leads.

Price and credit

The taste batch is $49. If you buy the pilot within 14 days of receiving the taste batch, the $49 is credited toward the pilot price of $490. There is no subscription and no contract.

Replacements, not refunds

A lead that misses the agreed brief, or whose source link is dead, is replaced. We do not offer refunds once a batch is delivered.

What we do not promise

We do not promise a number of replies, meetings or sales. Results depend on your offer and your outreach. We do not promise a specific number of leads: if your buyers do not describe the problem in public, we tell you before you pay.

How you use the leads

You may contact the people in your batch and keep the data. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to your outreach, including email, phone and messaging rules in the places you contact. Do not use the data to harass anyone or to resell the batch as a list.

Removal requests

If someone asks to be removed, we remove them from our data. If a lead in your batch asks you to stop contacting them, you must stop.
