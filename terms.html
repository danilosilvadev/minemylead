What you buy

A one-time niche pack: a SQLite file with about 100 A–C qualified leads for one niche, plus outreach drafts you consult in the desktop app. You keep the file. Each lead includes the public quote, a link to its source, a score, and public contact channels. Drafts are for you to edit and send. We do not send email, DMs, or InMail for you. Chat in the app reads the pack you already have. It does not mine new leads.

The Linux and Windows app is free to download and includes about 10 sample leads to consult. The optional taste is 10 qualified leads scoped to one niche. The taste is not the free sample set.

Price and credit

The niche pack is $199 once for one niche. There is no subscription and no monthly charge. The taste is $49. If you buy the pack within 14 days of receiving the taste, the $49 is credited toward that pack. Another niche, or a refresh of a niche, is another one-time pack at the same price unless we quote a different volume.

Replacements, not refunds

A lead that misses the agreed brief, or whose source link is dead, is replaced. We do not offer refunds once a pack, or a taste, has been delivered.

What we do not promise

We do not promise a number of replies, meetings, or sales. Results depend on your offer and on the messages you send. We do not promise thousands of leads, and we do not promise a specific count: about 100 A–C leads is the shape of a pack. If your buyers do not describe the problem in public, we tell you before you pay.

How you use the leads

You may contact the people in your leads and keep the data. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to your outreach, including email, phone, and messaging rules in the places you contact. Do not use the data to harass anyone or to resell the leads as a list.

Removal requests

If someone asks to be removed, we remove them from our data. If a lead asks you to stop contacting them, you must stop.
